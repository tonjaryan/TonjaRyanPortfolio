How I work

The stages I move through on a project, each linked to a place in the case studies where you can see it. Not every project went through every stage; the case studies say which ones did.

  1. Analyze

    Identify the performance problem, the audience, current conditions, and constraints, and separate what training can fix from what it can't.

    ExampleTSC needs and task analysis

  2. Design

    Write observable objectives, choose the instructional strategy, and set the assessment criteria before building.

    ExampleCo-Pilot design decisions

  3. Develop

    Build and revise the content, interactions, job aids, and reference material, with review rounds documented.

    ExampleFrom workbook to notebook

  4. Implement

    Coordinate delivery: schedules, rooms, platforms, stakeholder communication, and onboarding.

    ExampleFall 2025 operational rollout

  5. Evaluate

    Review evidence, find the gaps, and revise the training or the program around it.

    ExampleForum survey and debrief

Background

I'm the Supplemental Instruction Coordinator at the Sanger Learning Center at UT Austin. I oversee the Biology and Physical Sciences SI cohorts and manage the training, scheduling, and support of 50 to 70+ peer educators who lead weekly sessions across 13 undergraduate colleges, serving more than 2,500 students a semester.

I also work as a contract instructional designer with iDesign, writing course materials for partner institutions such as Dallas College.

Before UT, I was a lead science teacher in Austin ISD and a camp director at Lavner Education. I hold a master's degree in Educational Psychology.
