About Me

I design learning systems that help people build skill, confidence, and consistency in complex roles. My work combines instructional design, program management, and learning operations to turn training needs into usable materials, scalable workflows, and measurable program improvements.

At the Sanger Learning Center at UT Austin, I support a peer educator program serving approximately 2,500 students each semester. I manage training systems, coordinate cross-functional logistics, and develop AI-augmented learning tools that make peer education more consistent, scalable, and effective.

Instructional Design.

Learning experiences, interactive modules, workbooks, virtual trainings, and cohort tools designed to make complex content easier to teach, apply, and sustain. These projects combine evidence-based instructional design with AI-augmented workflows.

Instructional Design · Scenario Simulation

Reviewing
with the
Co‑Pilot.

RoleDesign, authoring & build
ForContent rating analysts · Trust & Safety
StatusIndependent work sample

A narrated simulation that teaches content-rating analysts to check an AI co-pilot's suggested age rating against the TV Parental Guidelines and MPA rules, then accept, modify, override, or escalate it and record the evidence behind the call.

ProcessGap analysis from the role, a two-part objective with six enabling objectives, four verification checks taught one at a time, then a six-case scored queue where a missed publication hold fails the attempt. Revised over documented review rounds; evaluation plan proposed, not yet run.
