About Me

I design training and the systems that support it, from analyzing performance needs and developing practical learning experiences to coordinating implementation and evaluating results. My work spans technical training, higher education, and large-scale program operations.

As Supplemental Instruction Coordinator at the Sanger Learning Center at UT Austin, I oversee the Biology and Physical Sciences cohorts and manage the training and scheduling of 50 to 70+ peer educators across 13 undergraduate colleges, serving more than 2,500 students a semester. I also design course materials as a contract instructional designer with iDesign.

Before UT, I was a lead science teacher in Austin ISD and a camp director at Lavner Education. I hold a master's degree in Educational Psychology.

How I Work.

The stages I move through on a project, each linked to the case study where you can see it. Not every project went through every stage; each case study says which ones did.

  1. 01

    Analyze

    Identify the performance problem, audience, conditions, and constraints, and separate what training can fix from what it can't.

    ExampleTSC needs and task analysis

  2. 02

    Design

    Write observable objectives, choose the instructional strategy, and set assessment criteria before building.

    ExampleCo-Pilot design decisions

  3. 03

    Develop

    Build and revise content, interactions, job aids, and references, with review rounds documented.

    ExampleFrom workbook to notebook

  4. 04

    Implement

    Coordinate delivery: schedules, rooms, platforms, stakeholder communication, and onboarding.

    ExampleFall 2025 rollout

  5. 05

    Evaluate

    Review evidence, find the gaps, and revise the training or the program around it.

    ExampleForum survey and debrief
